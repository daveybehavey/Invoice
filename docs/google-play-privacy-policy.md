# NoteBill Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-13

## Overview

NoteBill helps users turn rough notes, uploaded invoice content, and draft invoice details into professional invoices.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use NoteBill, how it is used, and what choices you have.

If you have a privacy question or request, contact `support@notebill.app`.

## Information We May Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

### Account and identity information

- Email address used for sign-in or account identification
- Internal account, session, or user identifiers used to keep invoices scoped to the correct user

### Content you provide

- Notes you type into the app
- Invoice details you create or edit
- Uploaded invoice files, text, or images
- Saved invoice drafts and related invoice metadata
- Audio notes and transcripts, if audio transcription is enabled

This content may include customer names, invoice amounts, line items, service descriptions, payment terms, and other business information you choose to include.

### Billing and payment-related information

If paid features or payment links are enabled, NoteBill may process:

- Billing-related customer identifiers
- Subscription status
- Customer email used for billing
- Payment-link and invoice payment metadata

Payment card details are generally processed by the payment provider and are not stored directly by NoteBill.

### Usage and diagnostic information

NoteBill may collect limited technical and diagnostic information to operate, secure, and improve the service, such as:

- Error and readiness information
- Import or OCR quality metrics
- Limited workflow and feature diagnostics

NoteBill does not use personal information for third-party advertising.

## How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Provide and operate NoteBill
- Generate, edit, save, export, send, and reopen invoices
- Process imports, OCR, and transcription workflows
- Authenticate users and associate invoice data with the correct account
- Provide subscription, billing, and payment-link functionality when enabled
- Send invoice emails or reminders when enabled
- Troubleshoot issues, maintain security, and improve reliability

## Service Providers

Depending on which features are enabled in production, NoteBill may use service providers to help operate the service. These may include:

- OpenAI, for AI-assisted invoice generation, OCR-related tasks, or transcription-related tasks
- Stripe, for billing, subscriptions, checkout, billing portal access, and payment-link functionality
- SMTP2GO, Resend, or another email delivery provider, for sending invoices or reminders by email
- Hosting, infrastructure, database, and related technical service providers used to run the app

These providers are used to operate the service and are not used to sell personal information.

## Sharing of Information

NoteBill does not sell personal information.

Information may be disclosed:

- To service providers that help operate NoteBill
- To comply with law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable government request
- To protect the rights, safety, and security of users, customers, or the public
- In connection with a business transfer such as a merger, acquisition, or asset sale

## Data Retention

Information is kept only as long as needed to operate NoteBill, provide requested features, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain necessary backups or security records.

Saved invoice drafts and related data may remain associated with your account until you delete them or request deletion, subject to backups and legal retention needs.

## Your Choices

Depending on how NoteBill is configured and where you live, you may be able to:

- Access or update information you provide
- Delete saved invoices from the app
- Request deletion of account-related data by contacting `support@notebill.app`
- Stop using optional features such as uploads, transcription, billing, or invoice email sending

If you request deletion, limited information may be retained where necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or backup purposes.

## Security

NoteBill uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information in transit and at rest.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## Children's Privacy

NoteBill is not directed to children under 13, and it is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to NoteBill, contact `support@notebill.app`.

## International Transfers

Information may be processed in countries other than your own, depending on where service providers and infrastructure are located.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If material changes are made, the "Last updated" date will be revised and additional notice may be provided when appropriate.
