# NoteBill Data Deletion Request

Last updated: 2026-04-13

If you want to request deletion of account-related data for NoteBill, email `support@notebill.app`.

## How To Request Deletion

Send an email to `support@notebill.app` with:

- Subject: `Delete my NoteBill data`
- The email address associated with your NoteBill account
- Any extra context that helps identify the correct account or saved invoice data

## What This Request Covers

A deletion request may include review of:

- Account-related identifiers
- Saved invoice drafts associated with your account
- Related account-scoped data stored to operate NoteBill

## Important Notes

- Some limited information may be retained when necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or backup purposes.
- Deleting a saved invoice in the app is separate from requesting account-level data deletion by email.
- If more information is needed to verify the request, NoteBill support may reply for confirmation.
