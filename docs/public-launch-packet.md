# NoteBill Public Launch Packet

## Core positioning

### One-line version

NoteBill helps service businesses turn rough job notes into clean invoices, statements, and follow-up fast.

### Stronger version

NoteBill is an invoice app for service work that helps you go from messy notes to a clean invoice, follow-up, and statement without a bunch of admin drag.

### Best "why now" version

I built NoteBill because too many invoice tools still make simple field-service billing feel slow, clunky, and weirdly stressful.

## Who it is for

- solo tradespeople
- cleaners
- handypeople
- small service operators
- anyone sending invoices from work they just finished

## What to emphasize

- faster from job notes to invoice
- cleaner mobile experience
- simpler repeat invoicing
- reminders, statements, and follow-up workflow
- clearer collections and repeat-client tools
- designed for getting paid, not just generating PDFs

## Offer framing

Keep the pricing story simple:

- Monthly Pro for people who want an easy ongoing subscription
- Lifetime Pro for people who would rather pay once and keep it

Best way to say it:

- "If you invoice every week, monthly is the easy option."
- "If you know you'll keep using NoteBill, lifetime is the one-and-done option."

Do not bury the offer under feature language. Lead with the workflow, then give the buyer a simple choice.

## What not to emphasize

- "AI" as the whole story
- giant automation claims
- enterprise-sounding language
- too many features at once

## Soft-launch CTA

Use:

- "Would love honest feedback."
- "Looking for a few early users to test it."
- "Curious whether this feels useful or confusing."

Avoid:

- hard sell language
- fake scarcity
- pretending it is bigger than it is

## FAQ answers

### What makes it different?

It is more focused on fast workflow, repeat client billing, and simple collections than generic accounting complexity.

### Is it only for one trade?

No. It is designed for service work broadly, especially solo and small operators.

### Is it mobile-friendly?

Yes. Mobile clarity and speed are a major focus.

### Is it replacing full accounting software?

No. It is focused on invoice workflow, getting paid, and reducing admin friction.

## Suggested launch order

1. publish the latest Android build
2. verify real-device billing when available
3. post organically in relevant communities
4. watch traffic + funnel events
5. tighten the message based on real reactions
