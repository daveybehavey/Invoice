## Full description

NoteBill helps freelancers, contractors, and small service businesses turn rough job notes into clean, itemized invoices without starting from a blank page.

Paste messy notes, import an old invoice, or reopen a draft you already saved. NoteBill organizes the details into a client-ready invoice, keeps the wording editable, and lets you review the money decisions before you save, export, or send.

What you can do in NoteBill:

- Turn rough notes into structured, send-ready invoices
- Review what Billie understood before you finalize the invoice
- Keep money decisions visible before anything is saved or sent
- Rewrite invoice wording while keeping totals visible
- Edit line items manually when you want more control
- Save invoice drafts and reopen them later
- Reuse prior services, wording, notes, and rates for repeat work
- Capture quick notes during the day and turn them into an invoice later
- Create hosted payment links and client portal links
- Send client statements and keep follow-up organized
- Import old invoice files, text files, PDFs, CSVs, JSON, and supported images
- Export invoices to PDF
- Track simple invoice states like draft, sent, opened, and paid

Who it is for:

- Independent tradespeople
- Freelancers and contractors
- Small service businesses
- Anyone who writes invoices from messy field notes

What NoteBill is not:

- Not full accounting software
- Not a bookkeeping platform
- Not a dashboard-heavy CRM
- Not a blank invoice template editor

NoteBill is built to keep invoicing simple: start with the work you already wrote down, review the money decisions clearly, keep repeat work organized, and send something professional faster.

Pricing framing for launch:

- Monthly Pro: the simple ongoing plan
- Lifetime Pro: the one-payment option for people who know they will keep using NoteBill
