# NoteBill Go-To-Market Plan

Updated: 2026-05-28

This plan is optimized for the fastest practical path to paid users:

1. direct intent traffic
2. a focused landing page
3. conversion tracking
4. compounding SEO content

## Primary goal

Get the first real monthly and lifetime purchases without wasting budget on broad traffic.

## Core offer

- Monthly Pro: the simple ongoing plan
- Lifetime Pro: the one-and-done option for people who know they will keep using NoteBill

## Paid traffic plan

### Campaign structure

Start with a single Search campaign focused on high-intent queries only.

#### Ad group 1: Invoice app

Keywords:

- invoice app
- invoice maker
- invoice software
- invoice generator

#### Ad group 2: Service work

Keywords:

- invoice app for contractors
- invoice app for service businesses
- mobile invoice app
- field service invoice app

#### Ad group 3: Collections and follow-up

Keywords:

- invoice reminder app
- client statement invoice app
- invoice follow up app
- get paid faster invoicing

### Match types

Use:

- phrase match
- exact match

Avoid broad match until you have enough conversion data to trust it.

### Negative keyword starter list

Add these early so the campaign stays high intent:

- free
- template
- pdf template
- examples
- jobs
- salary
- meaning
- definition
- accounting jobs
- accounting course
- bookkeeping course
- quickbooks
- xero
- excel
- word
- sheet

### Ad copy themes

Keep ads direct and practical:

- rough job notes to clean invoice
- statements and follow-up included
- built for mobile service work
- monthly or lifetime

## Landing page checklist

The landing page should answer these questions quickly:

1. What is it?
2. Who is it for?
3. Why is it better than a blank template or a generic accounting tool?
4. What does the paid plan unlock?
5. Why should I trust it?

### Best landing page layout

1. Hero
   - one sentence value prop
   - one short support sentence
   - primary CTA to start
2. Problem and outcome
   - messy notes in
   - clean invoice out
   - follow-up and statements built in
3. Product proof
   - screenshots of launcher, intake, library, and client workspace
4. Pricing choice
   - monthly
   - lifetime
5. Trust and FAQ
   - who it is for
   - what it is not
   - mobile-friendly
6. Final CTA
   - start free
   - upgrade when ready

### Landing page copy rules

- keep headings short
- keep paragraphs under 3 lines on desktop
- use one primary CTA repeated consistently
- keep “AI” language practical and secondary
- show statements and follow-up as part of the paid value

## SEO plan

Google’s SEO guidance favors helpful, people-first content and warns that results can take weeks or months to settle. That means SEO should compound in parallel with paid and direct outreach, not replace them.

### Priority pages

Create or strengthen pages around:

- invoice app for contractors
- invoice app for service businesses
- mobile invoice app
- client statements and invoice follow-up
- repeat client invoicing
- invoice reminders and collections

### SEO page structure

Each page should include:

- a clear title tag
- a unique meta description
- one main keyword theme
- short, scannable sections
- screenshots or product visuals
- internal links to the app and related pages

### Title tag pattern

Use this style:

- `NoteBill | Invoice app for contractors`
- `NoteBill | Client statements and follow-up`
- `NoteBill | Mobile invoicing for service businesses`

### Meta description pattern

Use one sentence that says:

- who it is for
- what it does
- the main benefit

Example:

- `NoteBill helps service businesses turn rough job notes into clean invoices, statements, and follow-up on mobile.`

## Conversion tracking

Primary conversion:

- `pro_unlock_verified`

Secondary conversions:

- `checkout_started`
- `billing_plan_selected`
- `account_signed_in`

If using Google Ads, import those events from GA4 and make `pro_unlock_verified` the main optimization target.

## Budget recommendation

Start small and learn fast:

- $10 to $20 per day
- 7 to 10 days
- only high-intent search

This is enough to see:

- whether clicks are relevant
- whether landing copy is clear
- whether people reach checkout or unlock

Do not scale until the campaign proves it can produce real conversions at a sane cost.

## What to avoid

- broad “invoice” traffic with no service intent
- broad display ads
- trying to buy SEO with ads
- burying the offer under too many features
- spending more before conversion tracking is clean

## Fast execution order

1. finalize landing page copy
2. publish the strongest launch post
3. launch the small search campaign
4. monitor conversion events
5. adjust keywords and negatives from search terms
6. write SEO pages based on the terms that actually convert
