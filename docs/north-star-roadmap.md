# NoteBill North Star Roadmap

Updated: May 29, 2026

## North Star

NoteBill should be the fastest mobile-first way for a solo service business to go from rough notes to a send-ready invoice, statement, and follow-up.

The product wins when it helps someone:
- capture work fast
- make a clean invoice without retyping everything
- review money decisions before anything is finalized
- send it
- follow up
- do it again faster next time

## What AI Should Do

AI should speed up the parts that are repetitive or language-heavy:
- turn rough notes into a first draft
- rewrite wording clearly
- transcribe voice notes
- extract text from photos or PDFs
- suggest safe wording improvements
- help users move faster through repeat work

AI should not make money decisions on its own.

## What the User Should Control

Users must stay in charge of:
- prices
- totals
- discounts
- tax
- final send decisions
- payment status changes

If money changes, the user should explicitly approve it.

## Core Product Wedge

The wedge is not “ChatGPT makes invoices.”

The wedge is:
- paste rough notes
- get a good first draft fast
- keep client memory
- send statements and follow-ups
- reuse the same client/work flow next time

That is harder to replace with a generic chat prompt.

## Three-Phase Roadmap

### Phase 1: Quick AI Invoice

Goal: make the fastest possible path from notes to draft.

What matters most:
- quick intake mode
- one obvious starting point
- simple review step
- mobile-first layout
- clean branded export

Success looks like:
- a user can paste notes and get to a draft fast
- the app feels like a shortcut, not a form maze
- the invoice looks professional enough to send

### Phase 2: Collections Loop

Goal: make NoteBill valuable after the first invoice.

What matters most:
- client statements
- reminders
- follow-up presets
- partial payment handling
- invoice history
- repeat-client memory
- reopen/resend/save/send loops

Success looks like:
- users come back because the app helps them get paid
- the dashboard shows what needs attention next
- follow-up feels calmer and more deliberate

### Phase 3: Growth and Monetization

Goal: turn the workflow into a repeatable business.

What matters most:
- clear monthly vs lifetime offer
- simple landing pages for high-intent search
- cheap search ads with strong intent match
- SEO pages that match real searches
- conversion tracking that maps to real product value

Success looks like:
- paid users come from real intent, not curiosity alone
- the funnel is measurable
- the offer is easy to understand

## What We Should Keep

Keep investing in:
- mobile-first speed
- branded invoice output
- custom logo and styling support
- client memory
- statements and reminders
- repeat-work shortcuts
- clear, low-friction pricing

## What We Should Not Drift Into

Do not turn NoteBill into:
- a generic accounting suite
- a logo design app
- a broad SMB platform
- an autonomous money-deciding AI
- a feature-bloated document editor

## AI Positioning

The best AI positioning is:
- “Billie helps you get from rough notes to a clean draft fast.”

Not:
- “AI does everything for you.”
- “ChatGPT, but for invoices.”

## Branding Support

Best practice for branding is:
- uploaded logo
- saved business colors
- style presets
- branded PDF/export output

We should keep logo support simple and reliable.
AI-generated logo concepts can be a later experiment, but they are not the core wedge.

## Metrics That Matter

Watch:
- first invoice completed
- first invoice saved
- first invoice sent
- first PDF exported
- second invoice created
- repeat-client reuse
- statement or reminder usage
- checkout started
- pro unlock verified
- paid conversion after repeated use

## Direction In One Sentence

NoteBill should feel like a calm, fast invoicing and collections assistant that helps solo service businesses get from notes to paid, without making them think about the tool more than the job.
