## Privacy Policy

NoteBill helps users turn messy notes, uploaded invoice content, and draft invoice details into professional invoices.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have when you use NoteBill.

If you have questions, contact us at `[support email]`.

## Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

### 1. Account and identity information

- Email address you provide when signing in or identifying your account
- Internal account or session identifiers used to keep your invoices scoped to you

### 2. Content you provide

- Notes you type into the app
- Invoice details you create or edit
- Uploaded invoice files, text, or images
- Audio notes you upload for transcription, if that feature is enabled
- Saved invoice drafts and related invoice metadata

This content may include customer names, invoice amounts, line items, service descriptions, payment terms, and other business information you choose to include.

### 3. Billing and payment-related information

If paid features or payment links are enabled, we may collect or process:

- Billing-related account identifiers
- Subscription status
- Customer email used for billing
- Invoice payment-link metadata

Payment card details are generally processed by our payment provider and are not stored by us directly.

### 4. Usage and diagnostic information

We may collect limited technical and diagnostic information to operate, secure, and improve NoteBill, such as:

- Error and readiness information
- OCR confidence or import-quality metrics
- Limited workflow or feature-usage diagnostics

We do not use this information for third-party advertising.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Provide and operate NoteBill
- Generate, edit, save, export, send, and reopen invoices
- Process uploaded files, images, and audio notes
- Authenticate users and keep invoice data associated with the correct account
- Provide billing, subscription, or payment-link features if enabled
- Send invoice emails or reminders if those features are enabled
- Maintain security, troubleshoot problems, and improve product quality

## Service Providers and Processing

We may use service providers to help operate NoteBill. Depending on which features are enabled in production, these providers may process data on our behalf.

These may include:

- OpenAI, for AI-powered invoice generation, transcription, or OCR-related tasks
- Stripe, for billing, subscriptions, checkout, customer portal, and payment-link functionality
- SMTP2GO, Resend, or another email delivery provider, for sending invoices or reminders by email
- Hosting, database, analytics, or telemetry infrastructure providers used to run the service

We use these providers to operate the app, not to sell your personal information.

## Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may disclose information:

- To service providers that help us run NoteBill
- To comply with law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable government request
- To protect the rights, safety, and security of our users, customers, or the public
- In connection with a business transfer such as a merger, acquisition, or asset sale

## Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to operate NoteBill, provide requested features, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Saved invoice drafts and related data may remain in your account until you delete them or request deletion, subject to backups and legal retention needs.

## Your Choices

Depending on how NoteBill is configured and where you live, you may be able to:

- Access or update certain information you provide
- Delete saved invoices from the app
- Request deletion of your account-related data by contacting `[support email]`
- Stop using optional features such as uploads, audio transcription, billing, or invoice email sending

If you request deletion, we may retain limited information where necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or backup purposes.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children’s Privacy

NoteBill is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact `[support email]` and we will review the request.

## International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, depending on where our providers and infrastructure are located.
